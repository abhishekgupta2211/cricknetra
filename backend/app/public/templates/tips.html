From better to best

Cricket tips

Sharpen every part of your game — practical tips for fielding, bowling, batting, keeping and captaincy.

🧤 1. Fielding

Watch the ball all the way into your hands. Keep fingers pointing down for low catches and up for high ones, give with the ball to cushion the impact, and "soft hands" beat snatching every time.

Stay low, attack the ball, and gather side-on so you're balanced to throw. Aim at the stumps from close range or hit the keeper's gloves over the bails from the deep. A direct hit wins races — back yourself.

Get your body behind the line as a second barrier, use the long barrier (knee down) on rough outfields, and attack the ball to cut the batter's running. Walk in with the bowler so you're moving as the ball is hit.

Point your front shoulder at the target, transfer weight from back foot to front, and release with a flat, low trajectory. Practise the crow-hop to add power without losing accuracy.

🎯 2. Bowling

Keep the shiny side out, point the seam toward fine leg, and angle your wrist behind the ball. A fuller length swings later and is hardest to drive — aim at off-and-middle.

Pick a target at the base of the stumps (or the batter's toes) and follow through hard. Practise to a shoe or marker in the blockhole. In death overs, a wide yorker is even harder to get under.

Spread the index and middle fingers across the seam, spin it off the index finger, and rip down hard for revolutions. Flight it above the eyeline to draw the drive, and use the arm-ball to surprise.

Hit the same channel — top of off stump — ball after ball. Dot balls force mistakes. Change pace and length only once you've made the batter commit to the front foot.

🏏 3. Batting

Get your head and front shoulder over the ball, present the full face of the bat, and drive through the line with high hands. Let the ball come to you — timing beats muscle.

Look for the single off every ball — push into gaps and call early and loudly. Soft hands into the off side and a quick first step keep the scoreboard ticking without risk.

Use your feet — get to the pitch to kill the turn, or go deep in the crease to use the extra time. Pick the length early, play with the spin, and keep a still head.

Break the target into overs, keep the required rate under control with ones and twos, and target one bowler for boundaries. Stay calm — wickets in hand let you accelerate at the death.

🧱 4. Wicket-keeping

Watch the ball out of the hand, rise with the bounce, and take it beside your body — never reach. Quiet feet and patience set up the stumping; let the ball come before you whip off the bails.

Start in a low crouch, watch the seam, and give with the ball into your body. Move your feet to get your head in line — catching is easy when you're balanced.

📋 5. Captaincy

Match the field to the plan — catchers in when the bowler's on top, save the boundary when you're defending. Build pressure with dots and let the field tempt the batter into your trap.

Keep your best bowler for the danger periods, rotate to break partnerships, and read match-ups (spin to the big hitter, pace to the nudger). Always hold an over in reserve for the death.
